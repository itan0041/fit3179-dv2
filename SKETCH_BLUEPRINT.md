# A4 Sketch Blueprint — "Grow Where You Are"

**Draw this by hand, on paper, in pen.** The rubric gives the sketch **0 marks if it was made with digital tools**, and a **−3% penalty if the sketch is for a different topic than the final page**. So this blueprint matches the page we are building, panel for panel.

Use **one A4 sheet, portrait**. Pencil first, then go over in black pen. Keep it clean and tidy — the HD descriptor is "four or more clear sections, headings, text, figures, with detailed, creative and varied charts and maps."

Also: the unit requires you to **acknowledge AI use** on the submission. We will draft that acknowledgement for you later.

---

## Overall page frame

Draw a **tall rectangle** on the A4 (leave ~1.5 cm margin all round). The page is **one scrolling column, 1180 px wide**, so sketch it as a narrow tall page split into **6 bands**. Rule a light horizontal line between each band and write the band number in the left margin.

Inside the bands, the page uses a **12-column grid**. Where a panel is half-width, draw it as half the band width with a gutter down the middle. Keep all panel left edges aligned on the same vertical line — the marker is explicitly looking for **sight-line alignment**.

---

## Band 0 — Title block (about 10% of the height)

- Big title, hand-lettered: **GROW WHERE YOU ARE**
- One line of subtitle underneath: *"Malaysia searched for gardening like never before in 2020. A decade of planting data shows what actually changed."*
- Small byline line: *Isaac Yeak Sern Tan · Monash University Malaysia · FIT3179 · October 2026*
- On the right of this band, draw **4 small boxes in a row** (KPI tiles). Label them:
  - `+156%` Kelantan veg. area
  - `−55%` Selangor veg. area
  - `2024` decade-high veg. area
  - `16.5 ha` Pahang, per 1,000 people

Write the word **"KPI tiles"** beside them so the tutor sees the intent.

## Band 1 — "The spike" (the hook)

- Heading: **A nation searched for gardening**
- One short paragraph block — draw 3–4 horizontal squiggle lines to represent body text, don't write it all out.
- **Chart 1 (full width): streamgraph.** Draw 4 stacked flowing bands that bulge dramatically around a point near the left-middle. Mark a vertical dashed line labelled **"MCO, Mar 2020"** at the bulge. Label the bands: *berkebun, gardening, hidroponik, kebun sayur*. Write **"streamgraph"** under it.
- Draw a small arrow annotation into the bulge saying *"peak interest"* and another at the right saying *"back to 2014 levels"*.

## Band 2 — "Did the ground follow?"

- Heading: **The attention faded. The acreage did not.**
- **Chart 2 (left half): slope graph.** Two vertical axes labelled `2014` and `2024`, with ~9 lines running between them, some rising, some falling. Label a rising one *Vegetables* and a falling one *Paddy*. Write **"slope graph"**.
- **Chart 3 (right half): bump chart.** A set of 5–6 lines that cross over each other left to right, with rank numbers 1–9 down the left edge. Write **"bump chart — category rank by planted area"**.
- **Chart 4 (full width): connected scatter.** A looping path of dots joined by a line, with a few dots labelled by year (2014, 2018, 2024). Axis labels: x = *planted area*, y = *production*. Write **"connected scatter"**. Add an arrow annotation at the lowest dot: *"2018 low point"*.

## Band 3 — "Where Malaysia actually grows" (THE MAP BAND)

> This band carries the map requirement. **Three or more map idioms or the Idioms mark is capped at half.** Draw them clearly and label each one with its idiom name.

- Heading: **Where the vegetables actually are**
- Draw the **shape of Malaysia** four times. You only need a rough outline: Peninsular Malaysia as a lumpy vertical blob on the left, and Sabah + Sarawak as a bigger blob on the right. Do not fuss over accuracy — just make it recognisable and consistent.
  - **Map 1 (left half): choropleth.** Shade the states in 4–5 different densities of hatching. Draw a small graded legend bar beside it. Caption: *vegetable land per 1,000 people, 2024*. Write **"choropleth"**.
  - **Map 2 (right half): proportional symbol map.** Same outline, but with **circles of clearly different sizes** sitting on each state — one very large circle on Pahang. Draw a nested-circle legend. Caption: *vegetable production, tonnes, 2024*. Write **"proportional symbol map"**.
  - **Map 3 (left half): bivariate choropleth.** Same outline shaded again, and beside it draw the signature **3×3 square colour-key grid** with arrows labelled *"searches →"* and *"actually grows ↑"*. Caption: *interest vs. production*. Write **"bivariate choropleth"**.
  - **Map 4 (right half): spike map.** Same outline with **vertical spikes/needles of different heights** rising out of each state, some pointing up and some down. Caption: *change in vegetable area, 2014→2024*. Write **"spike map"**.
- Add a callout arrow onto the Selangor/KL area reading: *"most people, least growing"*.

## Band 4 — "The urban paradox"

- Heading: **The states with the most people grow the least food**
- **Chart 5 (left half): dumbbell chart.** A vertical list of ~15 state names, each with two dots joined by a short horizontal line, arrow-headed. Some arrows point right, some left. Write **"dumbbell — 2014 vs 2024"**.
- **Chart 6 (right half): waffle chart.** A 10×10 grid of small squares, with one big block of ~36 squares shaded darkest and labelled *Pahang*, and the rest in lighter shades. Write **"waffle — share of national vegetable area"**.

## Band 5 — "When to plant"

- Heading: **No spring, no autumn — here, rain sets the calendar**
- **Chart 7 (full width): heatmap.** A grid, 12 columns (Jan→Dec) by 16 rows (states). Shade cells in varying density, darkest in a block around Nov–Dec. Draw the graded legend. Write **"heatmap — monthly rainfall by state"**.
- **Chart 8 (left half): radial / polar chart.** A circle divided into 12 wedges like a clock face with Jan at the top, and two outlines drawn on it — one roughly circular, one lopsided. Legend: *Malaysia* vs *Worldwide*. Write **"radial chart — seasonality"**.
- **Chart 9 (right half): dual-axis line + bars.** Bars rising towards the right end (rainfall) with a line crossing them going the other way (interest). Write **"rainfall vs. search interest"**. Annotate: *"Malaysians search least in the wettest month"*.
- **Chart 10 (full width): planting-window strip.** A horizontal band per region with a shaded "drier window" block highlighted somewhere along the 12-month axis. Write **"planting window by region"**.

## Band 6 — Footer

- A box containing small text lines labelled: **Data sources · Author · Date · AI acknowledgement · Licence**
